Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. This notice explains what VoiceBridge processes when you create an account, translate a voice note, or share translated audio.

1. Information processed

  • Account information: email address and authentication identifiers managed by Supabase Auth.
  • Voice recordings: audio files you record or upload, plus the translated audio generated for your request.
  • Text and language data: detected source language, target language, source transcript and translated transcript when returned by the provider.
  • Service records: job status, timestamps, delivery channel, recipient identifier, provider message reference and error details needed to operate the service.
  • Technical data: basic request and security logs generated by the hosting and database providers.

2. How voice audio is used

When you request audio-to-audio translation, VoiceBridge sends the selected recording to ElevenLabs Dubbing for processing. When you use Read a Voice Note, the uploaded recording is sent to the configured speech/transcription provider for transcription and text translation. Do not upload another person's voice unless you have their permission.

VoiceBridge stores source and translated audio in a private Supabase Storage bucket. The API creates short-lived signed URLs for playback; the current preview link lifetime is 30 minutes. The application stores job metadata and transcripts in its database so you can view translation history.

3. Messaging platforms

If you choose WhatsApp Business or Telegram delivery, the translated audio and recipient details are sent to the selected platform using credentials configured by the VoiceBridge service operator. WhatsApp Business messages are sent from the operator's registered Business number. Personal WhatsApp sharing is handed to the Android share sheet; you choose the recipient and confirm sending inside WhatsApp.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase provides authentication, database and private file storage.
  • ElevenLabs processes audio for the requested dubbing or speech tasks.
  • Render hosts the website and API.
  • Meta/WhatsApp and Telegram process data when you explicitly choose their messaging channels.

5. Retention and deletion

The current deployment does not implement an automatic audio-retention expiry or a self-service account/audio deletion control. Source audio, translated audio, transcripts and job records may remain in the private storage/database until the service operator deletes them. The operator should publish a dedicated privacy contact before opening the service to general users. Do not treat the current deployment as having automatic deletion.

6. Security

VoiceBridge uses authenticated API access, per-user job ownership checks, private storage, short-lived playback links and server-side provider credentials. These measures reduce exposure but cannot guarantee absolute security. Never share your password or provider tokens.

7. Your choices

You choose whether to upload or record audio, whether to request translation, and whether to share the result. You can stop before sending. For the moment, account and stored-audio deletion requires action by the service operator; self-service deletion is not yet available.

8. Contact and changes

This policy must be updated with the service operator's verified privacy/support email before public launch. The operator may update this notice as the product and its providers change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.
